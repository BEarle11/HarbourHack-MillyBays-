# Project Name

Milly Bays

# Project Description

It's difficult to know how to get rid of clutter in the house.
What if you could figure out how much you could sell a thing for, and then
produce an ad for it in one fell swoop?
What if there was also a super excited salesman to sell your product?

To do this we need to take an image, and then classify what's in it, and
estimate its current value. Then we should develop a transcript and turn it into
a video.

[GitHub Repository](https://github.com/BEarle11/HarbourHack-MillyBays-)

## Judging Criteria 

* Commercial and/or social value. Is this a business idea that could really work? Or is it something that does good for society? Or possibly both?
* Completeness of realization. How much did you build and at what level of quality? A demo can be a slide deck, but a web app is better.
* Depth of non-human collaboration. Was a non-human a serious contributor to your project? Creative or experimental approaches get bonus points.
